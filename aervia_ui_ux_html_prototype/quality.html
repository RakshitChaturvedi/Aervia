Quality flags

Flags are diagnostic; they do not all imply automatic exclusion.

FlagMeaningHandling principle
MISSINGRequired channel absentRetain row; exclude from calculations that require the missing value.
OUT_OF_RANGEValue outside configured boundsFlag and apply channel-specific eligibility rules.
FLATLINESuspiciously constant readingsReview sensor behavior and affected time interval.
SPIKERapid isolated changeDiagnostic by default; do not automatically discard a possible real plume.
RESTART / STARTUPDevice restart or warm-up periodPreserve provenance and gate analysis as configured.
SENSOR_FAULTSensor diagnostic indicates faultExpose fault and prevent unsupported interpretations.
HUMIDITYHumidity-related reliability contextShow context; do not claim correction unless a validated correction is applied.
CLOCK_UNSYNCEDTimestamp unreliable/unsynchronizedDo not invent wall-clock time.
SOURCE_REVIEWMalformed or suspicious source recordRetain raw source and mark for review.
GAPMissing interval in the seriesRepresent a gap rather than silently interpolating a reading.

Known limitation: baseline maturity
